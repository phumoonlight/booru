# 2.0.2

### Changed

- **A tag's category is free text.** New tag and the row editor take any word of letters
  instead of the five names in a dropdown; Artist, Copyright, Character, General and Meta
  are offered as suggestions, along with anything this board has already coined. They
  keep their colours and their place at the top of the Tags screen — a category the app
  doesn't know is drawn plain and listed after them.

### Fixed

- A tag carrying a category outside the five is listed rather than silently left out.
