# 1.0.7 — 2026-09-04

### Changed

- The upload queue is one card per row with the preview across the whole card, instead
  of a list with a small thumbnail beside the fields. The preview is sharp now, not a
  blown-up stamp.
- Reorder the queue with ↑ and ↓ on each card. Dragging by a handle is gone — it was
  awkward once a card filled the window.
- Uploaded images are now stored at most 2048px on either side. Larger images are
  scaled down to fit, so a post loads faster without looking any different on screen.
- Animations are the exception and are still stored at their full size.
